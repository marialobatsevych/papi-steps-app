## 📱 Key Features

### Evolution System
- 🥚 Birth: 0-10,000 steps
- 👶 Baby: 10,001-20,000 steps
- 🧒 Teenager: 20,001-30,000 steps
- 🐕 Adult: 30,001+ steps (daily goals)

### Stats System
- Decreases from 100 to 0 over 24 hours
- Continues even when app is closed
- Hunger 🍖 / Fun 🎾 / Energy ⚡

### Daily Bonus
- 1 food + 1 toy every 24 hours
- 9 food types, 6 toy types
- Auto-claimed on login

### Coin System
- Earn 300 coins per daily goal
- Starting balance: 0
- Future: Shop purchases
